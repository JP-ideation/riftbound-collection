 [Orlando Riftbound Showdown] 
 The Orlando Riftbound Showdown brought together 556 players in Orlando, Florida, on October 31–November 1, 2026, for a major Riftbound TCG tournament during the Radiance Set 5 season. Hosted by CoolStuffInc, the event featured on-site coverage by Runes & Rift. This was the second stop of the Radiance Riftbound Showdown Series, as the competitive Riftbound meta continues to evolve with the new Set.
 Explore the Riftbound Top 8 decklists and tournament results below to see which decks rose to the top of this $25K prize pool event. You can also watch replays of the live coverage on our YouTube channel: Orlando Riftbound Showdown Playlist
