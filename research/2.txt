 How to read this tier list
 Tiers run from S to D: S holds the Legends winning the most right now — the ones you expect at every tournament — while D holds the ones currently struggling, which does not mean unplayable, it means they ask more of you to go the distance. Order within a tier matters: higher is stronger. Click a card to open the recommended deck for that Legend, ready to copy into the deck builder. When a Legend appears twice, it has two deck variants with different game plans, each with its own page.
 This page is a judgement, declared as one: team Eclipse curates it from real tournament results. The rest of the numbers live in the three pages below.

 Frequently asked questions
 What is the strongest Riftbound deck right now?
 Tier S currently holds Riven Midrange, Kai'Sa Midrange, e Kennen Midrange. At the very top sits Riven Midrange. The answer moves with the meta: this page updates with every change, so what you read is the current state, not an old snapshot.
 How often is the tier list updated?
 There is no fixed schedule: it updates when the meta moves — after major tournaments, after a ban, after a set release. The latest change was on 1 November 2026, made by team Eclipse, and the exact date always shows under the title.
 Who decides the tiers?
 Team Eclipse, curating from official tournament results and direct competitive experience. This is not an algorithm's output: it is a signed judgement, and the signature sits at the top of the page.
 How is this tier list different from the tournament statistics?
 Statistics tell you what happened: how often a card shows up in winning decks. The tier list tells you what to expect now: it also weighs matchups, bans and where the meta is heading. Read the two together — one is the photograph, the other is the reading.
