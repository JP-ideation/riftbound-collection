 [Riftbound Showdown-LDXP Sacramento-Riftbound Top 8 Decklists] 
 The LDXP Sacramento Riftbound Showdown attracted 755 players , making it the first Riftbound Radiance Showdown. Hosted by Laughing Dragon Events in Sacramento, California, the tournament featured $25,000 USD in prizes . Players competed for Top 8 finishes and RQ and RC qualifications for the upcoming Riftbound Regional season. Explore the Riftbound Top 8 decklists below to see which decks performed best at the tournament!
