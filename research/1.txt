 [Riftbound Regional Championship Europe — Top 8 Decklists] 
 The Riftbound European Regional Championship brought together 315 of the best Riftbound players in Stuttgart, Germany, on November 6–8, 2026, for the first-ever Riftbound Regional Championship during the Radiance Set 5 season. Every competitor earned their invitation by finishing in the Top 64 at a Regional Qualifier, bringing together some of the strongest players in the world to compete for the inaugural Regional Championship title.
 The three-day event featured 12 Swiss rounds across Days 1 and 2, followed by a Day 3 Top 8 playoff, where the final eight competitors battled to crown the first-ever Riftbound Regional Champion.
 Explore the Riftbound Top 8 decklists and tournament results below to see which decks rose to the top of this historic championship. You can also watch replays of the live coverage on our YouTube channel: Riftbound European Regional Championship Playlist
